French Notes — Avoir vs. Être
AVOIR = “TO HAVE”

Use avoir when someone has or possesses something.

Examples:
J’ai un livre. → I have a book.
Tu as un chien. → You have a dog.
Elle a une robe. → She has a dress.
Nous avons une voiture. → We have a car.
Vous avez un chapeau. → You have a hat.
Ils ont des chaussures. → They have shoes.
Avoir is also used for age

In French, you say that you have a certain number of years old, not that you are that age.

J’ai 15 ans. → I am 15 years old.
Tu as 14 ans. → You are 14 years old.
Il a 16 ans. → He is 16 years old.

❌ Je suis 15 ans
✅ J’ai 15 ans

Avoir expressions

Some things that use avoir in French may use “to be” in English.

J’ai faim. → I am hungry.
J’ai soif. → I am thirsty.
J’ai chaud. → I am hot.
J’ai froid. → I am cold.
J’ai peur. → I am afraid.
J’ai sommeil. → I am sleepy.

So, when you are talking about having something, age, or certain expressions, use avoir.

ÊTRE = “TO BE”

Use être to describe who someone is, what someone is like, or where someone/something is.

Examples:
Je suis Najeeb. → I am Najeeb.
Tu es étudiant. → You are a student.
Elle est française. → She is French.
Nous sommes amis. → We are friends.
Vous êtes intelligent. → You are intelligent.
Ils sont sportifs. → They are athletic.
Être is used for descriptions

Use être when describing a person or thing.

Je suis grand. → I am tall.
Elle est petite. → She is short.
Il est intelligent. → He is intelligent.
La robe est verte. → The dress is green.
Les chaussures sont bleues. → The shoes are blue.
Être is also used for location

Use être to say where someone or something is located.

Je suis à l’école. → I am at school.
Elle est à la maison. → She is at home.
Le livre est sur la table. → The book is on the table.
Nous sommes au magasin. → We are at the store.
How to tell which one to use

Ask yourself:

Does it mean “to have”?

Use AVOIR.

J’ai un chapeau.
I have a hat.

Does it mean “to be”?

Use ÊTRE.

Je suis grand.
I am tall.

Is it someone's age?

Use AVOIR.

J’ai 15 ans.
I am 15 years old.

Are you describing someone or something?

Usually use ÊTRE.

Elle est belle.
She is beautiful.

Are you saying where someone/something is?

Use ÊTRE.

Il est à l’école.
He is at school.

Conjugation to memorize
AVOIR

J’ai — I have
Tu as — You have
Il/elle a — He/she has
Nous avons — We have
Vous avez — You have
Ils/elles ont — They have

ÊTRE

Je suis — I am
Tu es — You are
Il/elle est — He/she is
Nous sommes — We are
Vous êtes — You are
Ils/elles sont — They are
